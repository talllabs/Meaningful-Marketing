About WORTH

An industry where more women lead.

WORTH is a Canadian nonprofit founded in British Columbia. We champion self-identifying women and gender-diverse people working in recreation, tourism and hospitality.

Through education, mentorship, advocacy and industry partnerships, we help women build careers and work with employers to increase representation in decision-making roles.
