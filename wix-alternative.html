Why organizations move from Wix

When freedom becomes clutter.

Wix’s flexibility is a strength at the start. As a site grows, many teams find they need more structure, more custom development and more control.

01

Scalability

Each page is designed on its own canvas. With dozens or hundreds of pages, keeping layouts, spacing and styles consistent takes more and more effort.

Shared templates keep every page consistent automatically.

02

Content architecture

Programs, resources, staff profiles and events work best as structured content that can be reused, filtered and searched across the site.

We model your content so it can be organized many ways.

03

Custom development

App market add-ons cover common needs, but features specific to your organization are harder to build and maintain inside the platform.

We build exactly what your programs need.

04

Control and portability

A Wix site lives on Wix. The site itself cannot be exported as code and hosted somewhere else.

A custom site can be set up so you own the code and choose where it runs.

What a custom website provides instead

Structure, flexibility and control.

A custom website is built on a clear content structure: programs, events, people and resources are entered once and appear wherever they belong, in a consistent design.

That structure makes it easier to add new pages, search and filter content, and connect your website to the other systems your organization relies on.

Where it fits the project, your organization owns the domain, hosting accounts and source code, and can move them in the future.

  • Searchable directories
  • Interactive maps
  • Member and client portals
  • Program and resource libraries
  • Custom databases
  • Dashboards
  • Online payments and bookings
  • CRM and email integrations
  • API integrations
  • AI-powered website search and Q&A

Is moving right for you?

An honest fit check.

Wix may still be a good fit if:

  • Your site is small and one person manages the design
  • Apps from the Wix App Market cover what you need
  • You want an all-in-one builder with hosting included

It may be time to move if:

  • Pages look inconsistent and take a long time to update
  • You need content that can be filtered, searched and reused
  • You need integrations or features beyond the app market
  • You want to own your website’s code and where it is hosted

We are happy to tell you if staying put makes more sense.

What the Wix migration covers

Nothing important gets left behind.

Your existing website stays live while your new one is built. Here is what we take care of along the way.

Content migration

Wix does not offer a full-site export, so we carefully move your page content, blog posts, images and files, and restructure them into reusable content types along the way.

URL redirects and SEO protection

We map your current Wix URLs, including blog post links and older auto-generated page addresses, to their new locations so rankings and bookmarks are protected.

Forms and integrations

Wix Forms, bookings, stores and member areas are rebuilt or connected to the tools you use, and tested before launch.

Accessibility

We design and build to WCAG 2.2 AA standards with consistent headings, structure and keyboard navigation across every page.

Training

Your team learns how to add and update structured content, which is usually faster than designing each page by hand.

Launch support

We move your domain or point it to the new site, monitor search and analytics after launch, and stay available for questions.

Ownership

Your organization can own the source code, domain and hosting accounts, so the website is never locked to one provider.

Questions

Wix migration FAQ

Can we export our Wix site and move it ourselves?

Wix does not let you export a site as code to host elsewhere, so a move means rebuilding the site and migrating its content. We handle that for you.

Will our Google rankings drop when we leave Wix?

A careful migration protects them. We map and redirect your existing URLs, keep your page titles and descriptions, and monitor search performance after launch.

Can we keep our domain?

Yes. Whether your domain is registered with Wix or elsewhere, we transfer it or point it to the new site with no downtime.
