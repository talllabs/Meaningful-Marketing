Case Study — Website & Registration System

90's Summer
Day Camp

Turning a simple idea into a fully functional registration system for a small, private summer camp.

Client: Mia & Ashleigh Custom Website Registration Flow Parent Portal Admin Dashboard

Overview

A nostalgic vision,
made real.

Mia & Ashleigh came with a clear vision: create a small, nostalgic “90s kid summer” camp—slow, playful, outdoorsy—and make it feel legit from day one.

They didn’t want complicated software. They needed something simple that parents could trust and use easily.

The Challenge

Simple on the outside.
Solid on the inside.

They needed a site that could do a lot of things well—without feeling heavy or complicated to anyone using it.

At the same time, they were running this summer camp themselves—so it had to be easy to manage behind the scenes too.

  • Clearly explain the summer camp concept and weekly structure
  • Show limited availability (only 8 spots total)
  • Allow parents to register their child and select weeks
  • Encourage commitment with upfront payment options
  • Feel polished and real—without overbuilding

The Approach

Built around
how they actually work.

Instead of reaching for a bulky summer camp platform, we created a lightweight, custom website focused on clarity and ease—designed around how Mia & Ashleigh actually operate.

  • A simple homepage that communicates the vibe (90s-style, outdoors, creative play)
  • Clear weekly structure—7 weeks, Monday–Friday, 9–5
  • Straightforward pricing: $500/week or $3,000 for all 7 (discounted)
  • Visual cues to show limited availability, including pre-filled spots
  • Personal touches with photos of Mia & Ashleigh
  • Visual “feel” inspired by their reference site and vision board
  • Mobile-first design for busy parents

The Outcome

Simple works,
when it’s right.

A clean, trustworthy summer camp website that makes a small program feel established.

Parents can self-serve instead of texting and emailing back and forth.

Organizers have one place to manage everything—registrations, payments, and availability.

No ongoing software subscriptions or complex systems to maintain.

Key Takeaway

“You don’t need a heavy platform to run a summer camp well. When the system matches how you actually work, simple is better.”

Most importantly, it supports what they actually wanted: a simple, joyful summer camp experience—without operational chaos behind the scenes.
