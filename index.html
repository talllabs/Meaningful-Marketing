Featured AI Project

Bringing History to Life with AI

Client: AAA

The Brief

AAA asked Meaningful Marketing to bring their early 1900s founder to life for a live conference—using only a black-and-white photo. The goal: honor their legacy while showcasing bold innovation.

Our Approach
  • Used AI to turn one archival photo into a moving, speaking avatar—complete with historical voice and expressions.
  • Enabled real-time interaction, backed by custom knowledge about AAA's history and mission.
  • The avatar joined the keynote, answered live questions, and stunned the audience.
