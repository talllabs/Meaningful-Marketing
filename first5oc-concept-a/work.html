Our Current Work

Stronger systems. Brighter futures.

We work with families and partners across Orange County to improve how children experience health, early learning, and family support.

Our work starts with what families need.

Each focus area combines community voice, local data, cross-sector partnerships, and strategic investment.

Healthy beginnings

Health systems and family resilience

Connecting prenatal care, pediatric health, home visiting, behavioral health, and family support into a more coordinated experience.

Ready to learn

Early learning and child care

Expanding access to quality early learning and strengthening the child care providers who make it possible.

Family voice

Engaged neighborhoods

Supporting families as leaders in shaping programs, policies, and systems in the communities where they live.

Insight

Early Development Index

Using neighborhood-level school readiness data to understand strengths, reveal gaps, and guide shared action.

For dads

Fatherhood inclusion

Helping services recognize, welcome, and strengthen the important role fathers play in children’s development.

Everyday growth

Kid Builders

Giving families free, practical activities that support developmental milestones through everyday play.

Data becomes more powerful when communities can use it.

The Early Development Index helps partners understand how children are doing across neighborhoods. First 5 OC pairs those insights with family experience to turn information into meaningful action.
