About First 5 Orange County

Every child deserves a strong start.

We bring people, resources, and ideas together to help Orange County’s youngest children and their families thrive.

We focus on the years that shape everything.

The experiences children have from before birth through age five build the foundation for learning, health, and wellbeing. First 5 Orange County works across systems to make that foundation stronger for every family.

We listen to families, use local data, and partner with community organizations, public agencies, health systems, and educators to create practical solutions with lasting impact.
