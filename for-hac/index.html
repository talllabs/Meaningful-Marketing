Built Around Your RFP

Your Goals, and How We'll Measure Them

  1. Find resources quickly

    Navigation built around your audiences, not your org chart. Every report gets a tagged web page, and we test with real users before and after launch.

  2. Meet accessibility requirements

    WCAG 2.2 AA, verified with automated tools and manual testing: keyboard only, screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA, TalkBack) and zoom.

  3. Perform on all devices

    Light, fast pages designed for phones and slow rural connections, with Core Web Vitals in the "good" range.

  4. Staff publish on their own

    Ready-made blocks for pages, reports, trainings and news, plus hands-on training, a user manual and recorded how-tos.

  5. Credibility with funders

    A polished design built from HAC's brand standards, with your impact front and center.

  6. Grow engagement

    Newsletter sign-ups, training registration and downloads, each tracked in an analytics dashboard against today's baseline.

  7. Lower long-term cost and risk

    A supported, regularly patched platform with documented hosting and maintenance, and no unnecessary plugins.

  8. Adaptable and owned by HAC

    Easy to reorganize as funding and projects change. All deliverables, accounts and content belong to HAC.

New features from your scope of work

  • Central hub for consulting products: one place to browse services by audience and send a request, tracked for staff.
  • Searchable resource library: reports, toolkits and recordings filtered by type, topic and year, with accessible downloads.
  • Multimedia: videos with captions and transcripts, and charts with text descriptions and data tables.
  • Connected data sites: clear paths to Rural Data Central, Veterans Data Central and Heirs Central.
  • Analytics and SEO: dashboard setup, search-friendly pages, and old links redirected so nothing breaks.
