Loans & Funding

Capital for
rural homes.

Below-market loans and grants for organizations building and preserving affordable housing in rural communities.

Why HAC lending

Patient money for hard-to-finance places.

Banks often pass on small rural projects. Our loans fill the gap, with flexible terms and staff who understand rural development.

Start a loan inquiry ↗
Loan types

Find the right fit.

Sample products and terms for illustration. Staff manage these in the CMS.

  • Pre-development

    Get a project ready

    Cover early costs like site control, surveys and design before construction money is in place.

  • Self-help housing

    Families building together

    Site and construction financing for groups of families who build their own homes.

  • Rental

    Build or preserve rentals

    Construction and bridge loans for affordable rental homes, including preservation.

  • Site acquisition

    Secure the land

    Loans to buy and prepare land for new affordable homes.

  • Community facilities

    Places that serve everyone

    Financing for clinics, child care centers and other essential rural facilities.

  • Disaster recovery

    Rebuild after a disaster

    Fast, flexible capital to repair and replace homes after floods, fires and storms.
