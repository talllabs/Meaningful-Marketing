Chilhowie style guide

One simple guide for every staff member who updates the website. These colors, fonts and blocks are built into the editor, so every page looks like Chilhowie.

Colors

Inspired by the town seal and the mountains around it. Every text color passes WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (4.5:1 or higher). The numbers below are the real contrast ratios.

Mountain Green#1D5235Links, buttons, headings · 9.1:1 on white ✓
Deep Forest#0F2E1EHeadings, footer · 14.7:1 on white ✓
Autumn Orange#E8833AMain action buttons, with dark text · 6.0:1 ✓
Sage#7FAE95Decoration only. Never use for text (2.5:1 ✗)
Ink#18212FBody text · 16.2:1 on white ✓
Slate#4A5568Secondary text · 7.5:1 on white ✓

Font

Atkinson Hyperlegible. It was made by the Braille Institute to be easier to read for people with low vision. Letters that look alike, like I, l and 1, are easy to tell apart.

Il1 O0 rn m

Body text is 18px or larger. Lines are kept short and easy to follow.

Buttons

Three styles only. Every button is at least 48px tall, so it is easy to tap.

How we write

The editor checks these rules as staff type.

Write like you talk

Short sentences and everyday words. Aim for an 8th grade reading level.

Lead with the answer

Put the date, the cost or the phone number first.

Spell out short forms

Write "Virginia Department of Transportation (VDOT)" the first time.

Post web pages, not just PDFs

If you must post a PDF, the editor checks that it is accessible.

Make links say where they go

"Read the January agenda," not "click here."

Use the ready-made alert

One alert banner style for closures, road work and weather.
