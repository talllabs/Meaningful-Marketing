The Trail at a Glance

Not one trail — a living network.

Long before it carried mail, gold and the 1925 serum run, these routes were used by Alaska Native peoples for travel and trade. Today the Trail connects communities, recreation, stewardship and history across Southcentral, Interior and Western Alaska.

This homepage tells that story in one scroll: where the Trail is, how people use it, where it came from and who lives along it.

Generations

Alaska Native peoples travel and trade along these routes.

1908

The route from Seward to Nome is surveyed for a winter trail.

1910s

Gold rush era: mail, freight and gold move by dog team.

1925

The serum run carries diphtheria antitoxin to Nome.

1973

The first Iditarod Trail Sled Dog Race.

1978
