Built from the Trail patch.

The website's colors and type come straight from the Iditarod National Historic Trail logo: the navy and royal blue stitching, the sled-dog gold and the bold, sign-like lettering of "National Historic Trail."

Logo use

A few simple rules for staff and partners.

Pair it with the name

In the site header, the patch sits to the left of "Iditarod Historic Trail Alliance," centered on the name.

Give it room

Keep clear space around the patch at least as wide as the "I" in IDITAROD.

Don't recolor or stretch

Always use the full-color patch. Scale it evenly; never squash, rotate or outline it.

Works on light or navy

The patch has its own white edge, so it reads clearly on white, snow or Trail Navy backgrounds.
