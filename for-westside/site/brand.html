Westside style guide

One simple guide for everyone who edits the website. These colors, fonts and blocks are built into the editor, so every page looks like Westside.

Colors

Every text color passes WCAG 2.2 AA contrast (4.5:1 or higher). The numbers below are the real contrast ratios.

Westside Blue#1450A3Links, buttons, headings · 7.8:1 on white ✓
Deep Blue#0B2F66Headings, footer · 13.0:1 on white ✓
Sunshine#F6B21BMain action buttons, with dark text · 8.7:1 ✓
Wave Blue#2AA7DFDecoration only. Never use for text (2.7:1 ✗)
Ink#18212FBody text · 16.2:1 on white ✓
Slate#4A5568Secondary text · 7.5:1 on white ✓

Font

Atkinson Hyperlegible. It was made by the Braille Institute to be easier to read for people with low vision. Letters that look alike, like I, l and 1, are easy to tell apart.

Il1 O0 rn m

Body text is 18px or larger. Lines are kept short and easy to follow.

Buttons

Three styles only. Every button is at least 48px tall, so it is easy to tap.

How we write

The editor checks these rules as staff type.

Aim for a 6th to 8th grade reading level

Use short sentences. One idea per sentence.

Say "you" and "we"

Talk to the reader like a helpful neighbor.

Spell out acronyms

Write "Individual Program Plan (IPP)" the first time, or just "your plan."

Put people first

Say "a person with a disability," and "people we serve."

Make links say where they go

"Download the intake form," not "click here."

Use ready-made banners

Choose from three banner styles. No more one-off colors.
