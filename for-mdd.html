VIDEOS THAT CONNECT.

A selection of our video work, chosen specifically to show the storytelling approach we'd bring to the State of the Region bid.

Why these examples

We selected these pieces because they show the kind of storytelling we believe will work best for State of the Region: real people, real places, clear ideas, and an emotional connection to issues that can otherwise feel highly technical.

Andean Health & Development AHD Cotopaxi

Why we selected it: It demonstrates place-based impact storytelling around healthcare and community development. It shows how interviews, environmental footage, and a clear narrative can help an audience understand why a major regional investment matters to the people it serves.

Inspire 20th Anniversary

Why we selected it: This is interview-led storytelling with people who are not professional actors. The strength comes from authentic voices and personal experiences — the same style we'd bring to regional leaders, residents, employers, and community partners. This piece was created for Inspire's Annual Gala.

Lutheran World Relief Coffee Is Life

Why we selected it: It demonstrates our full documentary process, from concept and scripting through interviews, filming, music, and editing. It also shows how a broad economic and development story can become personal by focusing on the people behind it.

Andean Health & Development What We Do Every Day

Why we selected it: It shows how we take a complex organization and explain its work through real people and everyday moments. That's especially relevant to infrastructure, workforce, and regional development, where the challenge is making large issues understandable and meaningful.

Papua New Guinea Tourism Papua New Guinea

Why we selected it: It shows our ability to tell the story of a place through people, culture, and atmosphere. For State of the Region, that same approach can make Arkansas, Mississippi, and Tennessee feel tangible and connected, rather than relying only on statistics and presentation slides. This video uses no actors — it simply follows the PNG tourism rep around New York City, with the script built entirely from his own words.

U.S. Tour Operators Association (USTOA) Courageous Conversations

Why we selected it: Tasked with a creative concept for the conference theme "Courage," we reanimated historical icons — many with no original source audio or video — bringing them back to life to inspire a new generation of explorers. It shows the range of what we can do when a story calls for something bolder than a standard interview format, from concept and scripting through voice synthesis and immersive design.
