The Challenge

A platform holding
the team back.

Blues Backroads had a tourism website built on WordPress that had become harder for the team to manage. Updating events, itineraries, and other content took more effort than it should.

The team wanted a simpler system—without losing existing Google rankings or important content built up over years.

  • WordPress had become difficult for staff to update day to day
  • Managing events and itineraries took more effort than it should
  • Existing Google rankings and SEO equity needed to be protected
  • Important content couldn't be lost in the transition

The Approach

A full rebuild on
Squarespace.

Meaningful Marketing House rebuilt bluesbackroads.com on Squarespace, making the site much easier for staff to update day to day.

  • Migrated all existing content from WordPress to Squarespace
  • Protected important SEO to preserve existing Google rankings
  • Rebuilt itineraries and interactive maps
  • Streamlined the events section for easier management
  • Created a custom event submission system so local partners can send in events for review
