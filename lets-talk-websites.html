Blues Backroads · MidSouth Development District

The client needed
A regional tourism website on WordPress had become harder for the team to manage, and local partners needed an easier way to share events.
We did
We moved the site off WordPress to a custom platform, migrated the content, rebuilt itineraries and interactive maps, and created an event submission system where regional partners submit events for review.
The result
A tourism website that is easier to explore, easier to manage and ready to keep growing, with important SEO protected through the move.

BaldrigeAlliance.org

The client needed
The Alliance took on the national Baldrige Award process on top of supporting its network of state and regional programs, which meant new audiences, new services and a much more complicated story, all on a rigid WordPress site.
We did
We simplified the information architecture and rebuilt the site as a custom web application with an interactive member map, a program finder, a self-service member portal, an AI site assistant grounded in Alliance content and an admin portal with permission levels for staff.
The result
News, events, member records and media were migrated, every legacy URL was mapped to protect SEO, and the site soft-launched ahead of the annual conference.

TheSigmundProject.org

The client needed
A growing nonprofit serving several distinct audiences of tourism professionals needed member accounts, user-generated content and a way to fund its mission, all beyond what its Squarespace site could do.
We did
We moved the platform from Squarespace to a custom build with a member portal and user-generated content, and launched the RFP Hub, a paid product that connects tourism businesses with contract opportunities.
The result
A community of 10,000+ tourism professionals, and an earned-revenue product projected to bring in about $50,000 a year.

Adventure International

The client needed
Decades of destinations, travel brands and specialized experiences had piled up on a site where pages repeated themselves and the strongest stories were buried.
We did
We mapped how every brand and destination connected, then built one consistent page structure and reusable templates that fit very different offerings while still feeling like one organization.
The result
A clearer, easier-to-navigate site, carried through launch including the domain and DNS transition and post-launch testing.
