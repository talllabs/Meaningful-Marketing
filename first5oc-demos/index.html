Kid Builders

An activity finder for families with children ages 0–5.

  • All 171 real Kid Builders activities with their official artwork
  • Pick more than one child’s age at once
  • Filter by the six builder areas, or show only new activities
  • Speech & language activities and videos in English, Español and Tiếng Việt

Learning Link

A county-wide directory of play-and-learn locations.

  • Search by city, program or location
  • Filter by day, time, language and services
  • Switch between list and map views
  • Fall / Spring schedules staff can update twice a year

Real and illustrative content

Kid Builders uses First 5 OC’s current activities and artwork. Learning Link locations and schedules and every EDI number are invented to show how those experiences work.

Built to swap in real content

Sample data lives in separate files, so real content (or a CMS) can replace it without redesigning the pages.

Accessible by default

Keyboard-friendly controls, visible focus, screen-reader labels, sufficient contrast, and mobile layouts throughout.
