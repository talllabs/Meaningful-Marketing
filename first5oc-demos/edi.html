Early Development Index (EDI)

How ready are our kids for kindergarten?

Every few years, kindergarten teachers fill out a short checklist about each child in their class. Together, those checklists show how young children are doing across our county, and where families may need more support before kindergarten starts.

Why it matters

The first five years shape the next fifty.

Most brain growth happens before age five. The EDI doesn’t grade children; it gives communities a picture of what’s working and where to help.

01

Spot needs early

Neighborhood-level patterns show where families could use more support, years before gaps show up in test scores.

02

Guide investments

First 5 OC uses EDI results to decide where to focus programs, partnerships and funding. Confirm wording

03

Track progress together

Repeating the EDI in waves lets schools, cities and nonprofits see whether things are improving over time.
