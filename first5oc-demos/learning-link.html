Learning Link · Orange County

Find a Learning Link near you.

Learning Link is a drop-in play and learning program for children from infancy through age 5 and their parent or caregiver. Children meet other children while you learn new ways to play with and support your child, with help from a qualified preschool teacher.

What to expect at Learning Link

For your child

  • Play with other children
  • Fine and gross motor development
  • Early positive experiences at school

For you

  • New ways to play with and support your child
  • Connection with other parents
  • Questions answered by a qualified preschool teacher
  • Awareness of developmental milestones
