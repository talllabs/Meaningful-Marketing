Digital Revenue & Product Strategy

The Sigmund Project

The Sigmund Project wanted to create a sustainable revenue source that could help cover its nonprofit operating costs and reduce its reliance on donor funding. We developed the RFP Hub, a paid digital platform that connects tourism businesses with relevant contract opportunities — including business and revenue strategy, website and member portal development, technology and product management, RFP sourcing and publishing, email marketing, SEO, generative engine optimization, Google Ads, ChatGPT advertising, customer support and subscription management. Now in its third year, it is projected to generate approximately $30,000 in annual revenue, with approximately $50,000 forecast for 2027.
