Stock donations

Give appreciated stock

If you donate stock that has grown in value and that you’ve owned for more than a year, you can take a charitable deduction for its fair market value on the day you donate. You’ll also avoid capital-gains taxes on the increase in value.

Required minimum distributions

Give from your IRA

If you are over age 73, you must take annual distributions from your retirement account. Up to $105,000 of your annual RMD from IRAs may go directly to charity, so you avoid paying income tax on that amount.

Contribute to our endowment

Care for generations to come.

Our small organization has historically sustained operations year to year, donation to donation. As part of a long-term strategy for sustainability, the AHD Advisory Board established an endowment to fund lasting, intergenerational impact for rural Ecuador.

Please continue to prioritize gifts to our annual operations. If you can make an additional gift, or include AHD in your estate plans, we would be honored to talk with you.

Conduct research with us

An ambitious, broad research agenda.

We focus on the public health issues facing our communities, using both quantitative and qualitative research. Our partners include Notre Dame’s Eck Institute, the University of Wisconsin’s Global Health Institute, Bellarmine University’s Department of Anthropology and the Catholic University of Ecuador.

Our hospital in Santo Domingo has a full microbiology laboratory with PCR capability and a −80 freezer, serving subtropical and tropical communities of 70,000 and more than 400,000 people.

Elective for medical students

A month-long elective in Ecuador

Because our model empowers local staff, we don’t place U.S. volunteers at our hospitals, and we don’t currently have opportunities for high school or undergraduate students.

Spanish-speaking fourth-year medical students and residents may take part in AHD’s month-long elective ($1,500, plus room and board).

Update, Sept. 2023: The State Department has issued a travel advisory for Santo Domingo. We are not currently hosting visitors from the U.S.
