The AHD model

The only model of its kind in Latin America.

AHD is the only rural health provider in Latin America that delivers quality care based on a proven model of self-sufficiency and capacity building. We also offer the only residency program that trains rural family physicians, where local faculty train Ecuadorian physicians to work in their own communities.

We work with local government, with U.S. and Ecuadorian universities, and with donors around the world.

View our partners

How the hospitals pay for themselves

  • Fees for service
  • Payments from Ecuador’s Social Security Institute and Ministry of Health
  • Contracts with local companies
  • Contributions from local municipalities

So donations from the U.S. go toward expanding the model and our training programs, not day-to-day operations.

Quality health care

Two hospitals, 100% run by Ecuadorians.

Opened 2014 · Santo Domingo

Hesburgh Hospital

A full-service training facility with a 60-bed hospital, outpatient areas, specialty departments and a training center, serving an area where 400,000 mostly poor people live. HH serves every patient who comes through the door, and each day moves closer to financial break-even thanks to a public-private partnership with Ecuador’s Ministry of Health.

Hospital PVM

Our pilot project in an underserved community. Hospital PVM became financially self-sustaining by 2007 and remains 100% self-sustaining today. It provides high-quality care to an extended community of 80,000, has served more than 175,000 people, and is the largest private employer in town.

Family practice residency

Training Ecuador’s rural doctors, in Ecuador.

Fewer than half of doctors in Ecuador have residency training. AHD trains Ecuadorian doctors in rural Family Medicine through a rigorous, 3-year residency program in partnership with Ecuadorian universities.

Years 1–2

Learn

Residents train in rural family medicine at AHD’s hospitals.

Year 3

Teach

Third-year residents train the next class of doctors.

After graduation

Lead

Graduates work at an AHD hospital or another rural facility, and keep learning, researching and shaping policy.

Andean Health Institute

Measuring impact. Sharing what we learn.

The Andean Health Institute (AHI) systematically measures the impact of our work, studies the determinants of health and disease in rural Latin America, and shares lessons learned with the larger public health community.

1. Monitor & measure

Evaluate AHD’s health and education outcomes and impact.

2. Research

Study the principal public health threats to our communities, including perceptions of health and illness.

3. Policy leadership

Influence local, regional and global health practice and policy.

Current initiatives

  • Mosquito-borne illness research with the University of Wisconsin–Madison
  • Antibiotic resistance research with Notre Dame’s Eck Institute for Global Health
  • Práctica Familiar Rural, a Spanish-language online medical journal used across Latin America
