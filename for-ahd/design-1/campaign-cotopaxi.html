Why now

From a storefront clinic to a model for the world.

Over the last 27 years, AHD has grown from a small storefront clinic in Ecuador’s countryside into two innovative, largely self-sustaining teaching hospitals, a residency program to train local doctors, an indexed medical journal and a research institute.

For years we explored how to use that experience to significantly expand our impact. Campaign Cotopaxi is the answer.

The hospital

Care that’s so hard to find in the countryside.

The cornerstone of the campaign is a new 42-bed teaching hospital in the heart of the Ecuadorian highlands. Cotopaxi is a beautiful province, but among the poorest in the country, with many unmet health care needs.

  • Critical care for adults and newborns
  • High-risk obstetrics for pregnant moms and infants with complications
  • Surgical specialties: orthopedics, urology and neurosurgery
  • Hemodialysis for patients with kidney failure

Why Cotopaxi?

  • Among the highest poverty rates in the country
  • 80% of the province identify as indigenous
  • Limited adult ICU care and no neonatal ICU care
  • Minimal specialized surgical services
