Camp 2 · Training doctors

We don’t fly doctors in.
We grow them.

Ecuador’s first residency for rural family doctors: a rigorous 3-year program, run with Ecuadorian universities, that trains 60 doctors a year.

1

Learn

Residents train in rural family medicine inside AHD’s hospitals.

2

Teach

Third-year residents train the next class of doctors.

3

Lead

Graduates lead care in rural Ecuador, and keep researching and shaping policy.
