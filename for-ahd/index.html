What we do every day

A story that is nothing short of inspirational.

For over two decades, Dr. David Gaus has worked to provide quality health care to the underserved rural poor of Ecuador.

Since launching Andean Health & Development in 1997 with his mentor and co-chair, the late Fr. Theodore Hesburgh, David has built a sustainable approach to rural health: developing local talent, establishing two hospitals and clinics, and creating Ecuador’s first residency training program for rural doctors.

Self-sustaining by design

Your gift grows the model. It doesn’t just keep the lights on.

Our hospitals combine fees for service, public-private partnerships with Ecuador’s Social Security Institute and Ministry of Health, local contracts and municipal support. Hospital PVM has been 100% financially self-sustaining since 2007.

That means donations from the U.S. are used to expand the model and our training programs, not to pay for day-to-day operations.
