2025 recap

Around Lake Michigan, for Cotopaxi.

The 2025 ride was another great success. More than 30 riders rode along the Lake Michigan shores from Mackinac, Michigan down to Michigan City, Indiana, raising over $400,000 for AHD’s exciting project in Cotopaxi.

Thank you, donors and volunteers, for your contributions!
