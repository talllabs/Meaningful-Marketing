How You Can Help

Your support makes our work possible. There are many ways to get involved and help bring quality health care to communities in need.

Join the Hesburgh Legacy Society

Join a community of AHD donors and board members by including Andean Health in your will and estate. For questions about planned giving, contact info@andeanhealth.org.

Stock Transfers & RMDs

Donate appreciated stock you’ve held for over a year to deduct its fair market value and avoid capital-gains taxes. If you’re over 73, up to $105,000 of your annual IRA RMD can go directly to charity.

Contribute to our Endowment

The AHD Advisory Board established an endowment to fund lasting intergenerational impact for rural Ecuador. Please keep prioritizing annual gifts; contact us to give to the endowment as well.

Read the Book

Pick up Long Road from Quito, the inspiring story of Dr. Gaus and AHD, from University of Notre Dame Press.

Conduct Research with Us

Our agenda spans vector biology, tropical disease, antimicrobial resistance, mental health, medical anthropology and medical education. Have an idea? Email us.

Spread the Word

Host a fundraising event or a speaking engagement with Dr. Gaus, and share our posts on social media.

Elective for Medical Students

Spanish-speaking fourth-year medical students and residents may participate in AHD’s month-long elective ($1,500, plus room and board). Because our model empowers local staff, we don’t place U.S. volunteers at our hospitals.

Update, Sept. 2023: The State Department has issued a travel advisory for Santo Domingo. We are not currently hosting visitors from the U.S.
