The Challenge

Too many families in rural Ecuador still lack access to quality health care. The consequences can be devastating, especially for mothers and children.

Over a third of the world’s population lacks access to quality health care.

Rural Ecuadorian populations face a wide range of health care issues rooted in social, economic and political determinants. A major issue is the relative lack of access to high-quality, reliable health care. Outpatient facilities are inaccessible and the nearest hospitals are typically hours away in major cities, forcing sick villagers to overcome geographic, economic and cultural barriers to seek care.

Those who are willing and able to pay for health services would greatly prefer to receive quality care in their own community, if such facilities were available to them.

Primary health care has accomplished much. Nevertheless…

Maternal Health

Women continue to suffer and often die from pregnancy complications.

Children’s Health

Children continue to die at an alarming rate from infectious diseases.

Chronic Illness

Diabetes, cardiovascular disease and arthritis, now more common with an aging population, go untreated because most rural health workers can’t manage chronic illness.
