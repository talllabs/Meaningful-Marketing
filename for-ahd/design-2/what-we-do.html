The AHD Model

AHD is the only rural health provider in Latin America that delivers quality care based on a proven model of self-sufficiency and capacity building. AHD offers the only residency program that trains rural family physicians, where local faculty train Ecuadorian physicians to work in their own communities.

AHD’s facilities combine fees for service, payments from Ecuador’s Social Security Institute and Ministry of Health, contracts with local companies and contributions from local municipalities. This means donations from the U.S. are used to expand the model and training programs, as opposed to paying for day-to-day operations.

Quality Health Care: Hospitals Hesburgh & PVM

Hesburgh Hospital

A full-service training facility in Santo Domingo, opened in 2014, with a 60-bed hospital, outpatient area, specialty departments and a training center, serving an area where 400,000 mostly poor people live.

  • Residency program (60 doctors a year)
  • Simulation lab
  • Two operating rooms
  • Full ER
  • Pharmacy & laboratory
  • Isolation wards
  • Neonatal unit
  • Dental services
  • Ultrasound, CT & MRI
  • In- & out-patient wings

Hospital Pedro Vicente Maldonado

AHD’s pilot project, opened in 2000 and financially self-sustaining since 2007. Hospital PVM provides quality care to an extended community of 80,000, has served more than 175,000 people, and is the largest private employer in town.

Family Practice Residency Program

Fewer than half of doctors in Ecuador have residency training, and the vast majority of those physicians live and work in the major cities. AHD trains Ecuadorian doctors in rural Family Medicine through a rigorous, 3-year residency program in partnership with Ecuadorian universities.

Third-year residents train subsequent classes of doctors. After graduation, the doctors work at an AHD hospital or another rural facility in Ecuador, and continue to learn, research and engage in policy discussion.

Andean Health Institute

The AHI systematically measures the impact of AHD’s work, studies the determinants of health and disease in rural Latin America, and shares lessons learned with the larger public health community.

  • Monitor and Measure. Evaluate AHD’s health and education outcomes.
  • Research. Study the principal public health threats to our communities.
  • Policy Leadership. Influence local, regional and global practice and policy.
