A bold plan for the future

Over the last 27 years, AHD has grown from a small storefront clinic in Ecuador’s countryside into two innovative, largely self-sustaining, state-of-the-art teaching hospitals, a medical residency program to train local doctors, an indexed medical journal and a research institute. Campaign Cotopaxi will:

  • Build a new hospital in Cotopaxi Province
  • Broaden training to nurses, lab techs and hospital administrators
  • Partner with universities in Ecuador and the U.S. to expand research
  • Support the often-neglected rural hospital across the Global South
  • Join a national pilot project to improve rural surgical services

The new hospital

The cornerstone of the campaign is a new 42-bed teaching hospital in the long-neglected Cotopaxi Province, with critical care for adults and newborns, high-risk obstetrics, surgical specialties such as orthopedics, urology and neurosurgery, and hemodialysis for patients with kidney failure.

Why choose Cotopaxi?

  • Among the highest poverty rates in the country
  • 80% of the province identify as indigenous
  • Limited adult ICU care and no neonatal ICU care
  • Minimal specialized surgical services
