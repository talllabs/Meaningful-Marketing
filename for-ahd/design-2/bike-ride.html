The 2025 Ride

The 2025 Bike Ride was another great success, as more than 30 riders joined the AHCT. We rode along the Lake Michigan shores from Mackinac, MI, down to Michigan City, IN, all in support of AHD’s mission. The ride raised over $400,000 to help support AHD’s exciting project in Cotopaxi! Thank you, donors and volunteers, for your contributions!
