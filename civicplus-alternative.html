Is moving right for you?

An honest fit check.

CivicPlus may still be a good fit if:

  • Your team relies on CivicPlus modules and is happy with how they work
  • You prefer one vendor handling hosting, software and support
  • A predictable subscription fits your budget process best

It may be time to move if:

  • Residents struggle to find services, forms or meeting information
  • You need features the available modules do not offer
  • You want to own your code, content and data outright
  • Routine updates depend on vendor support

We are happy to tell you if staying put makes more sense.

What the CivicPlus migration covers

Nothing important gets left behind.

Your existing website stays live while your new one is built. Here is what we take care of along the way.

Content and document migration

Municipal websites carry years of agendas, minutes, ordinances, PDFs and forms. We inventory every document, keep what residents still need, archive what they don’t and organize the rest so it is easy to search.

URL redirects and SEO protection

We map existing CivicPlus page and document addresses to their new homes, so links in old emails, council packets and search results keep working.

Forms and integrations

Request forms, notification sign-ups, payment links and connections to the other systems your departments use are rebuilt or reconnected, then tested end to end.

Accessibility

Public-sector websites have to work for every resident. We design, build and test to WCAG 2.2 AA standards, including the documents we migrate.

Security and reliability

Secure managed hosting, role-based administrative access, backups and recovery planning, uptime monitoring, a privacy and security review, and analytics and search monitoring, planned from the beginning rather than added on launch day.

Training

Department editors learn the updates they make most often, with written guides for the rest, before the new site goes live.

Launch support

We coordinate the domain and DNS changeover, monitor the new site closely and stay available after launch.

Ownership

Where it fits the project, your organization owns the domain, hosting accounts, source code and content.
