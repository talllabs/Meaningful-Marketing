How a CivicPlus migration works

02

Decide what should move

A migration is a good opportunity to clean house. We identify what should be migrated, rewritten, consolidated, redirected or archived.

03

Design and build the new website

04

Migrate your content and functionality

07

Side by side

CivicPlus vs. a custom website

Both approaches serve a purpose. The right choice depends on how much flexibility, ownership and customization your organization needs.

CivicPlus

  • Government-specific proprietary platform
  • Standardized modules and workflows
  • Managed within the CivicPlus ecosystem
  • Customization within available platform capabilities
  • Ongoing vendor relationship and platform fees

Custom website

  • Designed specifically for your organization
  • Custom functionality and integrations
  • Flexible administrative tools
  • Greater design and development freedom
  • Potential for client-owned code and infrastructure
  • Technology that can evolve with your organization
