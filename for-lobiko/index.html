Homepage concepts

Four directions for a new Lobiko homepage

Each one uses Lobiko’s own logo, photos, film and Gift Catalog. Click into any homepage, then switch between them using the bar at the top.

  • Mockup 1

    Warm & hopeful

    Familiar and trustworthy. Built from the supplied homepage code, with Lobiko’s own words, structure, photos and intro film.

    • Framed hero photo with logo-color arcs
    • Impact numbers up front
    • Intro film, blog and Join Us

    Type: Fraunces + Nunito Sans

    View Mockup 1 →
  • Mockup 2

    Logo reveal

    Cinematic. Lobiko’s own video plays through a cutout of the logo on bright yellow, which grows as you scroll until the whole story opens up.

    • Scroll-driven logo cutout, up to 8×
    • Cycling “Working to bring…” headline
    • Programs and a Gift Catalog slider

    Type: Outfit + Source Sans 3

    View Mockup 2 →
  • Mockup 3

    Campaign

    A humanitarian campaign homepage: documentary photos, bold stacked headline blocks and yellow reserved for giving.

    • Full-bleed campaign hero
    • Where your donation goes
    • Lobiko Champions monthly giving panel

    Type: Roboto

    View Mockup 3 →
  • Mockup 4

    Her story

    A short documentary as a magazine feature: one day with Mama Micheline, told in her own photos with hand-drawn orange marks.

    • Cinematic portrait hero with brush underline
    • Scroll-revealed photos and pull quotes
    • Gift amounts at the end of the story

    Type: Archivo

    View Mockup 4 →

All four are built to WCAG 2.2 AA, work on phones and respect “reduce motion” settings. The video hero has a pause button.
