About Our Work

Lobiko invests in local changemakers who are working to improve life in their communities. Together, they expand access to education, healthcare, economic opportunities, and social support for children and families facing extreme poverty. These resources help communities break cycles of poverty and violence.

Our Commitment

Colonialism, exploitation, and marginalization have left a legacy of political instability, armed conflict, and widespread poverty in Congo. Despite the country’s natural wealth, Lobiko reports that over 70% of the population lives in extreme poverty.

Lobiko invests in people and communities. It partners with local leaders already supporting children, young people, and adults experiencing extreme poverty, connecting them with the resources to sustain their work.

Our Story

Lobiko builds on the experience of CongoVoice, which worked in the region from 2012 to 2019. Lobiko’s founder, a Congolese native and former CongoVoice board member, established the initiative to carry that work forward.

When children have the support to thrive, they help shape the social, economic, and cultural life of their communities.
