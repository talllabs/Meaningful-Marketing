Quiet heroes

Every day at the Sabuli Children’s Center, quiet heroes give their time, love and energy so that children can grow up healthy, safe and hopeful. One of those heroes cares for 100 children a day.

Walk alongside Mama Micheline through a single day, from getting her own children ready in the morning to making sure every child at the center has food, care and encouragement before the day ends.

Early morning

Waking her children

The day begins at home. Mama Micheline gets her own children ready, makes sure there is food to reheat from the day before and, today, buys malaria medicine for her youngest.

The commute

Ready for the day

A bucket shower behind her home, a packed purse and good mornings to the neighbors. Then she sets out for the center.

Arrival

Warm welcomes

It’s the school holidays, and the children rush to greet her with hugs and smiles. Their joy lights up the morning.

“Your support is what empowers Mama Micheline to care for every child, every day.”
— The Lobiko team
Mid-morning

Every need met. It takes a team.

Micheline makes sure supplies last the month, handing out soap for laundry and the day’s food while keeping the inventory. She checks in with every caregiver about the night, and any child who is ill goes to the clinic.

Afternoon

Joy & soccer

She watches as the children dive into soccer practice, their laughter echoing across the field.

Learning time

Encouraging young minds

Even during the summer break, Micheline makes sure the children stay engaged, with tutoring to keep their learning on track.

All day long

Smiles all around

Play is a source of learning and fun. You can’t spend time with these children and not find yourself laughing too.

“What’s not pictured is the many nights she comes to check on the children late in the evening.”
— The Lobiko team
Sunset

Waving goodbye

As the sun sets, Micheline waves goodbye to the children, her heart full, and heads home to her own family.
