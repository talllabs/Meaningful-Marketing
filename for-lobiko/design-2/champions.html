Become A Champion

Why Become a Lobiko Champion?

Consistent funding and support ensure that we can achieve our commitment to the communities we serve. Becoming a Lobiko Champion is one way of demonstrating your solidarity with the people that we serve. It serves as your commitment, as a champion, to advance the holistic development of children to lead a purpose-driven life of wellbeing, to equip a generation of leaders that empower transformational change, and to promote the necessary foundation for economic sustainability.

Our Financial Model

Lobiko is committed to supporting no more than 75% of the overall program costs from our Program Partners. To promote sustainability, Lobiko will work with our Program Partners to develop strategic plans to ensure that they can generate at least 25% of their overall operating budget.

Lobiko is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (Tax ID: 85-2563277). All donations in the United States are tax-deductible in full or in part. Your generous financial contributions empower us to achieve our mission.
