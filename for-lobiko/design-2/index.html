Working to bring Safe HomesHealing CareQuality EducationNourishing MealsMeaningful Work to children and families in Congo.

Working to bring safe homes, healing care, quality education, nourishing meals and meaningful work to children and families in Congo.

We partner with Congolese changemakers who are already transforming their communities, and give them what they need to do more.

Our partners in Gemena

Local leaders.
Lasting change.

Every program is led by Congolese changemakers. Lobiko connects them with the resources to carry out their own plans.

  • Home & care

    Sabuli Children Center

    75 children have a home, caregivers and daily meals, run by the Sanguma Foundation.

  • Nutrition

    Mama Monique Foundation

    Led by Mama Benie, the nutrition center feeds and cares for about 200 children.

  • Healthcare

    Judy Medical Clinic

    Highly subsidized care for more than 4,000 community members every year.

  • Sport & mentoring

    Gemena Football Academy

    Coach Song builds character through football for more than 200 young people.

  • Higher education

    Ubangi Protestant University

    The region’s first accredited university, serving 500 students.

  • Food & work

    Sabuli Growing Garden

    A 120-acre farm that feeds children and employs more than 500 people.
