What we do

About Our Work

We are committed to transforming lives by investing in community changemakers, ordinary people making extraordinary efforts to uplift their communities. By supporting these changemakers, we help create lasting opportunities for individuals facing extreme poverty, particularly children and families. Through education, healthcare, economic empowerment, and social support, we ensure that communities have access to resources needed to break cycles of poverty and violence.

Our Commitment

Due to a brutal history of colonialism, exploitation, and marginalization, Congo continues to grapple with deep-rooted challenges like political instability, armed conflict, and widespread poverty. Despite the nation’s abundant natural wealth, over 70% of the population lives in extreme poverty, while a small portion enjoys access to wealth and the opportunity for a life of well-being.

At Lobiko, we are committed to addressing these challenges not by investing in projects but by investing in people and communities to create sustainable change. Instead of providing services directly, we partner with individuals and communities leading efforts to support vulnerable children, youth, and adults in extreme poverty in Congo. Through this people-centered approach, we collaborate with those on the ground to transform their communities and support their success despite a legacy of exploitation and hardship.

Our Story

Lobiko builds on the proven experience, lessons learned, and successes of CongoVoice, an organization active in the region from 2012 to 2019. Our founder, a Congolese native and former CongoVoice board member who has led numerous initiatives in Congo, established Lobiko to carry forward this impactful work. With a renewed mission and strategy, Lobiko is dedicated to transformational change, drawing on past successes to achieve even greater impact in the region.

Children’s well-being directly contributes to their communities’ social, economic, and cultural fabric. As they grow, they become the architects of a better future, and their success is intricately interwoven with the progress of the places they call home.
