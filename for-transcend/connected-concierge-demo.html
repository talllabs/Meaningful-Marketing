Left: what a corporate event planner sees in chat on transcendcruises.com. Right: the agent working across the deployment calendar, NetSuite and HubSpot in real time, the same tools the team now bridges by hand.

The proof point Alaska Shore Tours connected an AI agent to their reservation system through an MCP server. Mid-chat, it checks a guest's cruise schedule and drops a ready-to-book proposal. They measure AI spend against staff minutes saved and see roughly a 9,000% return. And 80% of their customers book within 24 hours, so a fast answer wins the sale. This demo applies that pattern to Transcend's charter business.
