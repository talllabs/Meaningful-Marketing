Entertainment

Stages for every kind of moment.

03

The Forum

This magnificent two-story theater is the focal point of the ship. Tiered seating, unobstructed views and state-of-the-art audiovisuals make every spot the best seat in the house.

  • 755 ft² / 70 m²
  • 120 guests
  • Two-story screen
04

Vine Lounge & Bar

Enjoy a drink or dance under the moonlight as the music lingers into the early hours. Vine Lounge moves gracefully from formal event space to casual cocktails.

  • 2,449 ft² / 278 m²
  • 120+ guests
  • Grand piano · DJ booth
05

The Studio

A canvas for your vision, tailored to your brand or celebration. Two 75-inch 4K screens make it ideal for presentations, breakouts and intimate gatherings.

  • 1,300 ft² / 121 m²
  • 80 guests
  • Flexible space
06

The Sundeck

Soak in the sun as the historic European landscape drifts by. From daytime celebrations to nighttime screenings, it is the perfect venue for brand and product launches.

  • 4,766 ft² / 443 m²
  • 120+ guests
  • 144” retractable screen
