About

Hi, I’m KC. I’m a creative director and storyteller, and for most of my career I’ve told stories for brands that want to make a difference in the world. I can point to that at every stop along the way.

It started at TOMS, where I joined as a media intern and stayed nearly ten years, growing into Media Content Manager, leading video across every platform and managing teams of editors, cinematographers and photographers. Since then I’ve been creative partner to TOMS founder Blake Mycoskie, led content at Madefor, served as Creative Director at WiddyUP and, most recently, been Director and Head of Video at K12.

Along the way I’ve lived and worked in London, Los Angeles, Bangkok, Sacramento, Jackson Hole and now Ōmokoroa, New Zealand. What ties it all together is stories that get people to care, and then do something about it. That’s the work I want to keep doing.
