About

Hi, I’m KC. For most of my career I’ve told stories for brands that want to make a difference in the world.

It started at TOMS, where I joined as a media intern and stayed nearly ten years, eventually leading video across every platform. Since then I’ve partnered with TOMS founder Blake Mycoskie, led content at Madefor, been Creative Director at WiddyUP and Head of Video at K12. London, LA, Bangkok, Jackson Hole, and now Ōmokoroa, New Zealand.
