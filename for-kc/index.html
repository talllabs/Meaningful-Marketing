About

I started at TOMS as a media intern and spent nearly ten years there, growing into the person who led video across every platform.

That’s where I learned how a story can move people to buy, give and join in. I went on to be creative partner to TOMS founder Blake Mycoskie, led content at Madefor, served as Creative Director at WiddyUP and, most recently, Director and Head of Video at K12.

I’ve lived and worked in London, Los Angeles, Bangkok, Sacramento, Jackson Hole and now Ōmokoroa, New Zealand. I’m drawn to work with a purpose, and I bring the same craft to any brand ready to say something worth hearing.
