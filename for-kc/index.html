About

Hi, I’m KC. I’m a creative director and storyteller. I use words, images and film to build brands and ideas that move people.

I spent nearly ten years at TOMS, starting as an intern and growing into Media Content Manager, leading video across every platform and managing teams of editors, cinematographers and photographers. From there I became creative partner to TOMS founder Blake Mycoskie, led content at Madefor, served as Creative Director at WiddyUP, and most recently was Director and Head of Video at K12.

I’ve lived and worked in London, Los Angeles, Bangkok, Sacramento, Jackson Hole and now New Zealand. What ties it all together is stories that get people to care, and then do something about it.
