Meet Alan Elliott

Curiosity
& connection

Alan Elliott began his career as an educator. Consulting work soon drew him to the travel and tourism industry, where he built Myriad into an international marketing company.

Today, he combines that experience with a belief in collaboration through The Sigmund Project, speaking, advisory work and service on boards around the world.
