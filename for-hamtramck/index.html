Homepage concepts

Three directions for a new Hamtramck Parks homepage

Each one uses the Conservancy’s own logo, photos and video. Click into any homepage, then switch between them using the bar at the top.

  • Mockup 1

    Warm & cinematic

    Familiar and welcoming. A full-screen film of the parks, clear paths to explore, and every page in the main menu ready to click through.

    • Background video with a pause button
    • Six full pages, from Parks to Get Involved
    • Events with list and month views

    Type: Manrope + DM Sans

    View Mockup 1 →
  • Mockup 2

    Logo reveal

    Elegant and editorial. The park film plays through a cutout of the leaf logo, which grows as you scroll until the whole scene opens up.

    • Scroll-driven logo cutout, up to 8×
    • Cycling “Working to bring…” headline
    • Park list with hover photo previews

    Type: Cormorant Garamond + Figtree

    View Mockup 2 →
  • Mockup 3

    The world in two square miles

    Bold and joyful. Hamtramck’s mix of languages and cultures takes center stage, with color, stickers and a lot of personality.

    • “Welcome” in six languages
    • “Pick your vibe” park finder
    • Parks as admission tickets

    Type: Bricolage Grotesque + Instrument Sans

    View Mockup 3 →

All three are built to WCAG 2.2 AA, work on phones, and include a pause button for any moving video or animation.

Homepage concepts prepared by Meaningful Marketing. Photos, logo and video belong to Hamtramck Parks Conservancy; events and some text are samples.
