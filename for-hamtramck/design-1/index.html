About & Impact

Rooted in place.
Growing together.

We work with neighbors and partners to steward Hamtramck's parks, preserve its heritage and open doors to recreation.

Why we're here

Everyone deserves a place to belong.

Hamtramck Parks Conservancy is a community-focused nonprofit that supports parks and green spaces through collaboration, advocacy, programming and improvements.

Working alongside the city, schools, volunteers and community partners, we help make these spaces welcoming and useful for the people who rely on them.
