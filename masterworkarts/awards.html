Our awards program

Three tiers of support, one shared standard

Everything we fund — from a $500 community grant to a $10,000 commission — aspires to a level of excellence that only earnest dedication to a craft provides.

$500–$1,000

Community Arts Grants

We support community-level arts programming designed to inspire and encourage continued development through the arts, because we believe in the value the arts bring to building community.

In the past we've funded everything from a wildly popular chorale experience at Carnegie Hall to new theatrical productions to an award-winning film about 9/11.

$500

Emerging Artist Award

Designed for an individual who possesses a unique dedication to their craft — something we appreciate across all disciplines.

$10,000

Premier Award

New to our re-imagined vision, the Premier Award funds a new work developed by either an individual artist or an arts organization. It represents Masterwork's commitment to new work across four disciplines that rotate annually — music, theater, dance, and the visual arts.

How to apply

Look for details in our journal and on social media

Applications for each award tier open at different points in the year and are announced through our journal and social channels. For details on eligibility, deadlines, and how to apply, reach out directly.
