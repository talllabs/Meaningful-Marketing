Our programs

Three ways we support artists

From first community showcases to major new commissions, our grants meet artists where they are.

$500–$1,000

Community Arts Grants

Funding community-level arts programming designed to inspire continued development through the arts, from a Carnegie Hall chorale to an award-winning 9/11 documentary.

$500

Emerging Artist Award

Recognizing an individual artist with a unique dedication to their craft, across any discipline.

$10,000

Premier Award

Supporting a major new work by an individual artist or arts organization, rotating annually across music, theater, dance, and the visual arts.

Our story

Started by one woman's belief in the arts

Masterwork began more than 60 years ago in Morristown, New Jersey — the brainchild of Shirley May, who understood the importance of nurturing developing artists while enriching the cultural life of the community. Since her passing in 2016, we've recommitted to expanding her vision for a new generation of artists.
