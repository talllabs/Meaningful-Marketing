Our story

Started by one woman's belief in the arts

Masterwork began more than 60 years ago in Morristown, New Jersey — the brainchild of Shirley May, who understood the importance of nurturing developing artists while enriching the cultural life of the community. Since her passing in 2016, we've recommitted to expanding her vision for a new generation of artists.
