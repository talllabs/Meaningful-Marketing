Our reason for being

We believe in the arts — and in the vision Shirley May had for supporting excellence in the arts at every level.

A foundation built on one woman's belief in artists

Masterwork Music and Art Foundation began more than 60 years ago in Morristown, New Jersey — the brainchild of Shirley May, who was passionate about the arts and understood the importance of nurturing developing artists while enriching the cultural life of the community. Generations of artists were shaped by the years of concerts, theatrical events, and fine arts projects Masterwork funded or produced.

In 2016, Shirley passed, prompting a revitalization of our organization and a recommitment to expand her vision. Today, we're intent on growing our reach and impact to meet the changing needs of today's developing artists.
