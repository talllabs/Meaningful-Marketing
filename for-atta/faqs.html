Membership FAQs

A clearer path to your benefits.

Quick answers for joining, getting started, bringing your team, and renewing.

Your questions, answered.

Design note

FAQ answers are rewritten from ATTA’s published FAQs to match the new journey (HUB-first sign-in, category kept through account access, Summit follow-up in the HUB).

How do I access the Member HUB?

Use your ATTA account to sign in. You’ll go straight to the HUB, with account-access help beside each first step.

Why do I need a free account first?

ATTA requires a free community account for access to purchased products. Your selected organization category stays selected through account access.

Can my employees use our membership?

Membership grants HUB access to all employees of the member company. Each employee creates an individual HUB account with a company email address. Contractors can be added with ATTA support.

Which courses and research are included?

Business Membership includes two OnlineEDU courses per year: Adventure Travel Safety & Risk Management and one course of your choice.

Where do I renew?

Choose Sign In to Renew, then review your membership summary and current terms.

How do I stay connected after a Summit?

Open Summit Follow-up in the Member HUB for event discussions, session resources, delegate networking routes, and future gatherings.

Who can help me choose a membership?

Our membership team is happy to help. Start by comparing membership options to see categories, dues and eligibility.
