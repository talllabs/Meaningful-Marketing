Membership FAQs

A clearer path to your benefits.

Quick answers for joining, getting started, bringing your team, and renewing.

Your questions, answered.

How do I access the Member HUB?

Use your ATTA account to sign in. This proposed flow routes members directly to the HUB and offers account-access support beside each first step. The prototype uses a sample account.

Why do I need a free account first?

ATTA requires a free community account for access to purchased products. In this concept, your selected organization category stays selected through account access.

Can my employees use our membership?

Membership grants HUB access to all employees of the member company. Each employee creates an individual HUB account with a company email address. Contractors can be added with ATTA support.

Which courses and research are included?

Business Membership includes two OnlineEDU courses per year: Adventure Travel Safety & Risk Management and one course of your choice. Confirm current access details with ATTA.

Where do I renew?

Choose Sign In to Renew, then review your membership summary and current terms. The renewal screen in this prototype shows sample usage only.

How do I stay connected after a Summit?

Open Summit Follow-up in the Member HUB for event discussions, session resources, delegate networking routes, and future gatherings. These are proposed journey improvements.

Who can help me choose a membership?

Visit the live membership site for current categories, dues, eligibility, and contact options: https://membership.adventuretravel.biz/
