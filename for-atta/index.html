About this mockup

A member-journey concept for ATTA, shown as it would look to the public. Every page in the menu and footer is mocked up; anything that would leave for another site has been pointed at the closest mocked page instead.

Pages: Home, Membership, Join / Renew, Tour Operator Membership, Member HUB, Membership FAQs, Member Stories, Chile 2025 Summit, Québec 2026 Wrap-up, Andalucía 2027 Registration, Seattle AdventureCONNECT, plus a tailored renewal summary (Member HUB → My Membership).

Decisions for a pilot:

  • Ownership: decide who owns the journey from Summit attendee to active member (Membership + Events).
  • Measurement: start with a baseline. Track early benefit use, post-event actions and first-year renewal by member category.
  • Validation: confirm dues, plan inclusions, employee eligibility, event details, content permissions and account integrations before launch.
