FAQ

Membership questions.

The questions prospective and current members ask most.

How do I get into the Member HUB?

Sign in with your ATTA account and you’ll land straight in the HUB, with account-access help beside each first step.

Why do I need a free account first?

A free community account is your key to anything you purchase from ATTA, membership included. Pick your organization category once and it stays selected while you finish up.

Can my whole team use our membership?

Yes! Every employee of a member company can create their own HUB account with a company email address. Contractors can be added with help from ATTA.

Which courses and research are included?

Business Membership includes two OnlineEDU courses every year: Adventure Travel Safety & Risk Management, plus one course of your choice.

Where do I renew?

Choose Sign in to renew. You’ll see a summary of your year, then your current dues and terms before you confirm.

How do I stay connected after a Summit?

Head to the Member HUB for event discussions, session resources, delegate networking and your next chance to meet up.

Who can help me choose a membership?

Our membership team is always happy to help. Start by comparing the options to see categories, dues and eligibility.
