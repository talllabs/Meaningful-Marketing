Our why

Travel should leave places better than we found them.

ATTA’s mission is to empower the global travel community to protect natural and cultural capital while creating economic value that benefits both trade members and destinations.

  • 5 to 10% less carbon per event delegate, every year
  • 1% for the Planet with the Adventure Travel Conservation Fund as nonprofit partner
  • 100% of Business Members formally committed to sustainability: the goal
Adventure Travel Conservation Fund

Founded with industry leaders in 2016, ATCF funds grassroots, community-led conservation projects protecting the places that make adventure travel possible. Members help choose the grant recipients each year.

FAQ

Membership questions.

The questions prospective and current members ask most.

How do I get into the Member HUB?

Sign in with your ATTA account and you’ll land straight in the HUB, with account-access help beside each first step.

Why do I need a free account first?

A free Community account is your key to anything you purchase from ATTA, membership included. Pick your organization category once and it stays selected while you finish up.

Can my whole team use our membership?

Yes. Business Membership covers one business and all of its employees. Each employee creates their own HUB account with a company email address, and contractors can be added with help from ATTA.

Which courses and research are included?

Business Membership includes two OnlineEDU courses every year: Adventure Travel Safety & Risk Management, plus one course of your choice. Many research reports are free for Business Members, including the quarterly Pulse of the Adventure Travel Industry.

How much does membership cost?

Tour operators and accommodations pay $350 to $1,250 USD a year based on annual revenue. Gear suppliers pay $700, industry partners $1,000, and tourism boards $1,000 to $2,500 depending on whether they’re a city, state or national board. Compare categories.

Where do I renew?

Choose Sign in to renew. You’ll see a summary of your year, then your current dues and terms before you confirm.

How do I stay connected after a Summit?

Head to the Member HUB for event discussions, session resources, delegate networking and your next chance to meet up.

Who can help me choose a membership?

The ATTA Membership Team is happy to help. Send us a question and we’ll get back to you by email.
