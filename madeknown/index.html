ABOUT US

Made Known Pictures

Made Known is comprised of filmmakers, designers, and content strategists who love storytelling. We make commercials, mini-docs, founder profiles, explainer videos, and branded content. We love helping people tell their unique and personal stories, extending their reach through the power of video.

Made Known Pictures was founded in 2007 by Scott Blanding and Brad LaBriola with the mission to produce hidden stories that should be made known to a wider audience. They have made commercials, mini-documentaries and promotional films for apparel companies, cooking apps, and non-profits supporting coffee farmers in Kenya. Made Known's filmmaking has helped organizations tell their unique story and gain recognition with their key audiences.

MEET THE FOUNDERS
DP, Writer, Director

Scott Blanding

A life-long film lover, Scott gradually combined his concern for social justice with his passion for film. Most notably, Women in War Zones (Best Documentary, 2009 Philadelphia Film Festival) — a documentary he shot and directed with Brad about two victims of sexual violence treated at Panzi Hospital in the DRC. After growing up in Roanoke, VA, Scott pursued filmmaking in Nicaragua, the Czech Republic and the USA, graduating from Temple University in Philadelphia. He now lives in LA, serves as a reservist in the Marines, and continues working with Women In War Zones International and Made Known Pictures.

Producer, Writer, Editor

Brad LaBriola

Brad's love for storytelling started with books. With the mentorship of Robert Pledge, CEO of Contact Press Images, Brad has produced films on four continents in five languages. From 2010–2012, Brad earned his MFA at the distinguished UCLA Producers Program, mentored by Oscar-winning producers including Peter Guber, David Hoberman, Barbara Boyle, Bobby Moresco, and Tom Nunan. His commitment: to produce deeply-felt stories that bring meaning to the confusion of our lives and renew a sense of wonder in humanity. What's your story?
